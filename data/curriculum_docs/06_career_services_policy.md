# Career Services: Placement Readiness and Partner Matching Policy

> Synthetic Sprints policy content created for this project.

## Readiness status
Each learner has a readiness status stored in the learner database: Ready for Placement, In Progress, Needs Review, or Dropped. Only learners marked Ready for Placement are eligible for partner openings.

## Eligibility rules for a partner opening
A learner is matched to an opening only when all of the following are true, using live database records:
1. The opening is in the learner's bootcamp track.
2. The employer is on the learner's preferred employer list.
3. Completed modules meet the opening's minimum.
4. The Python assignment score meets the opening's minimum score.
5. The learner's target salary in EGP does not exceed the top of the opening's salary band.

If any rule fails the opening is not recommended, and the reason can be shown to the learner.

## Partner tiers
Fawry is a tier-1 partner. Any recommendation involving a tier-1 partner is paused for human approval by a career advisor. Swvl, Vodafone Egypt and e& Egypt are standard partners.

## What advisors and agents must not do
Do not promise a job, an interview or a salary. Do not describe qualifications that are not in the learner record. Do not share one learner's data with another learner.

## Salary expectations
Target salaries are monthly gross figures in Egyptian pounds (EGP). Openings list a salary band in EGP. A learner whose target is above the band is told the opening is below their target rather than being hidden from the process.
